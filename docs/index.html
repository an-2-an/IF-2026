Faculty of Engineering and Physics

Foundry production, welding, materials science — the foundation of enterprises in the metallurgical and machine-building industry.

Mission of the Faculty of Engineering and Physics is to train specialists for the development of mechanical engineering and metallurgy in Ukraine and around the world.

The extensive system of machine-building and metallurgical enterprises of our region means a significant need for a large number of highly qualified specialists. Graduates of our faculty are «you» with metals: they thoroughly understand the structures, technologies of obtaining and developing metals and materials, understand the equipment and technologies of foundry production, welding technology and equipment, know how to restore and increase the wear resistance of parts and structures, as well as develop new technologies of foundry production of ferrous and non-ferrous metals and alloys.

The Faculty of Engineering and Physics is the basis of the machine-building and metallurgical industry of the Zaporizhzhia region and Ukraine!

The faculty was founded in 1930.
